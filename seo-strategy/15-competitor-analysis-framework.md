## 15.2 What we track (monthly)

| Signal | How | Measured or estimated |

| New content they publish | Sitemap diff month over month | Measured |
| Organic rank on google.com.pk | **Not automated** (Google blocks automated results). Only manual checks from Pakistan, recorded with date, device and location. | Measured manually |
| Mentions in lists, press, directories | Manual search | Measured |
| Their traffic | **Not tracked.** Third-party traffic estimates are not reported as fact. | — |

## 15.3 Reading rules

- Compare like with like: Maps signals against Maps rank, website signals against organic rank.
- A competitor outranking us with a weaker website means the gap is Profile, reviews or links. Don't respond with more content.
- Keyword-stuffed listing names: record them with screenshots. The owner decides whether to report.
- Never copy competitor text, photos or claims.

## 15.4 Quarterly review questions

1. Did any competitor enter or leave the Maps top 3 for our terms?
2. What changed for them (category, reviews, photos, new pages)?
3. Which of their content earns links or mentions that we could earn legitimately?
4. Has anyone launched a page type (catering, corporate, décor) that targets a gap we also have?
