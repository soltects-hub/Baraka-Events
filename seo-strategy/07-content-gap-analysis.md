# 07 · Content gap analysis

The principle: **improve existing pages before creating new ones.** The site has 53 URLs and 29 posts. The gap is mostly depth and ownership, not quantity.

## 7.1 Gap types found

| Type | Gap | Evidence | Fix | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Missing page** | Catering landing page not live | "catering services lahore" 1,000/mo. /catering returns 404 on production. | Release the existing page (release candidate) | High |
| **Ownership** | Service pages lose their own terms to the homepage | 23 queries in Search Console | Depth plus internal links (docs 06, 14) | High |
| **Intent unanswered** | Price-intent queries ("charges", "rates", "cost") | 4 queries at positions 43–78 | Refresh the cost guide with factors and owner-confirmed ranges only | High |
| **Keyword absent from titles** | Décor terms on the nikkah, barat, walima and birthday pages | Planner 210–1,300 | Title edits (approval) | Medium–High |
| **Stale** | "2025" in two titles (venues, walima décor) | Venues post 66 impr at pos 30.3 | Refresh, keep the URLs | Medium |
| **Thin** | About 10 posts at 400–530 words (the "baraka-events-…" series and trend posts) | Live crawl | Expand the ones with demand. Leave the rest. Merging only with redirects and approval. | Medium |
| **Proof** | No case studies with real event, venue, scope and photos | Content inventory | 2–3 owner-supplied case studies (new pages under /portfolio) | High for conversion and AI trust |

## 7.3 What we will not create

- More "best event planner in Lahore" posts. Two already exist, and the homepage owns the term.
- Area doorway pages.
- Posts on off-target queries ("event planning classes near me").
- Posts with invented statistics, prices or client stories.
