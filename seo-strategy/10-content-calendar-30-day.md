## Seasonality note

Pakistan's main wedding season runs through the cooler months. The calendar front-loads wedding and décor content for that reason. This is general market knowledge, not a measured figure; Search Console impressions will confirm or correct it.

## Publication recommendation format (per piece)

Every brief and draft ends with: **Publish / Revise / Hold**, the reason, the target page it supports, and the facts the owner must confirm.
