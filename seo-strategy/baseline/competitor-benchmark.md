## What the evidence says

1. **Tulips Events is the benchmark.** It ranks #1 in Maps and #1 in ChatGPT, has about 5× Baraka's reviews, and has a 439-URL content library heavily weighted to décor and wedding-function ideas (décor 135 URLs). It wins on volume of reviews **and** volume of topical content.
2. **a2z Events Solutions wins with a thin website.** It has 5 sitemap URLs, but 173 reviews, top Maps rank, a Shadiyana listing with published package prices, and heavy citation in AI web search. This shows **reviews + directories outweigh website depth** in this market.
3. **Baraka's advantages are real but underused:** joint-highest rating (5.0), the richest structured data in the set, a Gulberg III location shared with the leaders, and published per-head catering prices (no competitor publishes these on its own site).
4. **Baraka's measurable gaps:**
   - reviews (40 vs 63–199);
   - directory listings (none found; Shadiyana and Wezoree are cited by AI);
   - press and listicle mentions (none found);
   - décor-idea content depth vs Tulips;
   - a Maps category not aligned to "event planner" and "wedding planner".
5. **Keyword-stuffed Maps names** ("Event Planner Lahore", "Best Party Planner in Lahore") occupy top-10 Maps slots. They are reportable under Google's guidelines; owner decision.

## Monthly benchmark routine

Re-capture the Maps ranks (same method), ChatGPT queries 1–3 (logged-out, same wording), review counts and directory presence. Record changes in the weekly report (doc 16, section 7).
