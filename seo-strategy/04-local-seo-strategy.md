## 4.3 Why competitors outrank Baraka (evidence-based reading)

- **Not website quality.** Moments & Memories and Lamhey have no website. Lajpal's homepage has 139 words. All outrank Baraka.
- **Not location.** Tulips, a2z, Lajpal and Peony are in Gulberg like Baraka.
- **Category alignment matters.** Wedding-planner-category listings rank higher for "wedding planner lahore". Baraka ranks best on the term matching its own category.
- **Review volume** (measured 24 Nov via ChatGPT's map data): Baraka 40 vs Tulips 199, a2z 173, Black Tie 195, HJ 70, H&K 65. ChatGPT states it ranks by ratings and review counts. Ratings are similar everywhere (4.6–5.0).
- **Keyword-stuffed names** ("Event Planner Lahore", "Best Party Planner in Lahore", "Best Event Planner", "Kids Birthday Planner") break Google's guidelines but currently rank. Reporting clear violations through "Suggest an edit" or the Business Redressal Complaint form is legitimate. Claude prepares a shortlist with evidence, and the owner decides.

## 4.4 Genuine review programme

1. After every completed event, send the client one WhatsApp message with the direct review link (Profile → Ask for reviews).
2. Timing: 2–5 days after the event, when photos are being shared.
3. Template, to be personalised by the owner:
   > Assalam-o-alaikum [Name], it was an honour to be part of [event]. If you were happy with our work, a short Google review would mean a lot to our team: [link]. JazakAllah.
4. Never offer discounts or gifts for reviews. Never write reviews for clients. Never ask staff or family to review. Never gate (ask only happy clients first).
5. Track per month: reviews received, average rating, replies sent (doc 16).

## 4.6 Location-relevant content (no doorway pages)

- **Do:** venue guides for real venues Baraka has worked at, and event-type guides with genuine Lahore logistics (season, traffic and timing, farmhouse and marquee considerations).
- **Don't:** "Event planner in DHA / Johar Town / Bahria Town" pages with swapped place names. Only consider an area page if there is genuinely unique substance, such as real events in that area with photos.
